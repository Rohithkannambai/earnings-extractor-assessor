Investcorp Credit Management BDC, Inc. Announces Financial Results for the Quarter Ended

September 30, 2026

Investcorp Credit Management BDC, Inc. (NASDAQ: ICMB) (“ICMB” or the “Company”) announced its financial results today for its fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026.

HIGHLIGHTS

 

  •  

During the quarter, the Company had net advances of $0.7 million on delayed draw and revolving credit commitments to portfolio companies.

 

  •  

The weighted average yield on debt investments, at fair market value, as of September 30, 2026, was 9.53% compared to 10.51% as of June 30, 2026.

 

  •  

Net asset value decreased $0.42 per share to $3.02, compared to $3.44 as of June 30, 2026. Net assets decreased by $6.0 million, or 12.16%, during the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to June 30, 2026.

 

  •  

ICMB’s investment adviser waived all management fees earned for the quarter and waived the remaining $0.2 million of previously accrued income-based incentive fees payable.

 

  •  

Company maintains portfolio flexibility amid market volatility through sizable exposures to first-lien and floating rate instruments.

Portfolio and Investment Activities

The Company received proceeds of $1.9 million from repayments and amortization during the quarter.

 

1

During the quarter, the Company had net advances of $0.7 million on delayed draw and revolving credit commitments to portfolio companies.

The Company’s net unrealized gains and losses accounted for a decrease in the Company’s net investments of approximately $3.0 million, or $0.20 per share. The total net decrease in net assets resulting from operations for the quarter was $4.8 million, or $0.33 per share.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s investment portfolio consisted of investments in 31 portfolio companies, of which 85.56% were first lien investments, 14.32% were equity, warrants, and other investments, and 0.12% was unsecured debt. The Company’s debt portfolio consisted of 97.3% floating rate investments and 2.7% fixed rate investments.

Capital Resources

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $18.8 million in cash, of which $9.2 million was restricted cash, and $10.1 million of unused commitment under its revolving credit facility with Capital One, N.A. (the “Capital One Revolving Facility”).

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had availability to borrow $5.5 million from the revolving credit facility based on the borrowing base.

Prior Period Corrections

In the Form 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the Company identified and corrected errors related to the assessment of gross income for purposes of the gross income test under Subchapter M of the Code for the tax years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024 resulting in tax adjustments of approximately $0.9 million and $1.1 million, respectively. After filing its June 30, 2026 Form 10-Q, the Company identified additional information that increased the tax adjustments by approximately $1.2 million for 2025 and $0.1 million for 2024, which have been corrected in the September 30, 2026 Form 10-Q.

Net Asset Value to Adjusted Net Asset Value

In the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the Company recorded a Tax Liability under Section 851(i) associated with the tax periods ending December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 totaling $2.0 million, which was partially offset by a related reduction in incentive fee payable of $0.1 million. Also in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, the Adviser waived $0.6 million of management fees earned and agreed to waive $0.2 million of previously earned incentive fees as well as $1.1 million of future management and incentive fees (the “Waiver of Future Management and Incentive Fees”) to the extent such fees are earned.

In the quarter ended September 30, 2026 the Adviser waived the previously earned incentive fees of $0.2 million, as announced, as well as an additional $0.6 million of management fees earned in the quarter. The Adviser plans to waive future management and incentive fees up to the remaining $0.6 million to the extent earned. The Waiver of Future Management and Incentive Fees are not reflected in the Company’s reported net asset value (“NAV”) as of September 30, 2026. If the Company were able to recognize the Waiver of Future Management and Incentive Fees as of September 30, 2026, NAV would have increased by $0.6 million.

Subsequent Events

Subsequent to September 30, 2026 and through November 11, 2026, the Company received approximately $11.5 million from the repayment of five positions, including two full repayments. As of November 11, 2026, the Company had investments in 29 portfolio companies.

On October 13, 2026, the Company paid in full the additional tax (i.e., the Section 851(i) Tax Liability) based on the excess amount of nonqualifying income for the taxable years ended December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 in the amounts of approximately $1.2 million and $2.1 million, respectively.

On November 4, 2026, the Company repaid $10.0 million of outstanding borrowings under its Capital One Revolving Financing.

About Investcorp Credit Management BDC, Inc.

The Company is an externally managed, closed-end, non-diversified management investment company that has elected to be regulated as a business development company under the Investment Company Act of 1940. The Company’s investment objective is to maximize the total return to its stockholders in the form of current income and capital appreciation through debt and related equity investments by targeting investment opportunities with favorable risk-adjusted returns. The Company seeks to invest primarily in middle-market companies that have annual revenues of at least $50 million and earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization of at least $15 million. The Company’s investment activities are managed by its investment adviser, CM Investment Partners LLC. To learn more about Investcorp Credit Management BDC, Inc., please visit www.icmbdc.comU43T.
