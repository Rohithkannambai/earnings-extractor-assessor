Corvex Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

ARLINGTON, Va., Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Corvex, Inc. (Nasdaq: MOVE), an engineering-led AI computing platform specializing in GPU-accelerated infrastructure for AI workloads, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The third quarter is the Company’s first full reporting period following the June 30, 2026 transfer of the legacy healthcare assets. Prior-year periods reflect only the legacy healthcare business and are therefore not directly comparable.

 

"We are building the Corvex AI Factory business one underwritten project at a time, and the third quarter moved that business forward. We have now secured all of the approximately 8 MW of critical IT capacity in the plan we set out in August. Of that capacity, 1.5 MW is live and supports approximately $22 million of contracted ARR. The remaining capacity, at the Mid-Atlantic 1 expansion and Midwest-1, is on track to be ready for service in the fourth quarter of 2026. We see customer demand that meets our underwriting criteria, and we are working to complete offtake for a 1.6 MW NVIDIA Blackwell cluster planned for Mid-Atlantic 1 and for 4.5 MW at Midwest-1, where we also hold a right of first refusal on a further 12.5 MW in 2027. We finance each deployment alongside our customers and lenders to clear its cost of capital, and we continue to advance new data center opportunities in our pipeline," said Jay Crystal, Co-Founder and Co-Chief Executive Officer of Corvex.

 

“Corvex is an engineering company first. In the third quarter we launched Corvex Token Factory on schedule and received notices of allowance for two patents covering Corvex’s technology, bringing our portfolio to two allowed patents and 10 pending patent applications. The goal is to get more useful work out of every GPU and every watt, on infrastructure customers can trust with their most sensitive data,” said Seth Demsey, Co-Founder and Co-Chief Executive Officer of Corvex.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:

 

●Total revenue for the third quarter was $5.0 million, up 31% from $3.8 million in the second quarter of 2026. Revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $9.3 million.

 

●Deferred revenue, including current and non-current portions, was $3.2 million at September 30, 2026, compared with $12,000 at December 31, 2025, reflecting amounts collected in advance for contracted AI compute capacity not yet recognized as revenue.

 

●Net loss attributable to common stockholders for the third quarter was $(17.5) million, or $(0.60) per share. Net loss attributable to common stockholders for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $(35.4) million, or $(3.22) per share.

 

●Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, was $(3.2) million for the third quarter and $(8.0) million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Adjusted EBITDA for AI Platform and services was $(3.2) million for the third quarter and $(5.6) million for the nine-month period.

 

●Total stock-based compensation expense was $11.3 million in the third quarter, including $9.3 million recorded in general and administrative expense.

 

●Cash and cash equivalents were $42.0 million at September 30, 2026. Cash used in operating activities for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $7.0 million.

 

 

 

Business Highlights:

 

●Contracted annualized revenue on live compute was approximately $21.7 million as of November 12, 2026. Corvex defines this operating metric as the annualized value of fixed contractual fees on capacity that has been delivered, accepted by the customer and is generating revenue as of the stated date. It excludes contracted capacity that is not yet live, is not a forecast, and is not a GAAP financial measure.

 

●All AI Platform and services revenue today is generated under take-or-pay contracts rather than spot pricing, meaning that customers reserve compute and storage capacity under those agreements and pay the contracted fee regardless of utilization.

 

●Corvex launched Corvex Token Factory publicly on September 30, 2026, moving the serverless inference platform out of closed alpha as planned. Developers and enterprises can register at no cost, obtain an API key, and run open-weight models with zero data retention by default.

 

●On August 31, 2026, Corvex announced plans to grow critical IT capacity from approximately 1.5 MW to approximately 8 MW by the end of 2026, doubling capacity at its Mid-Atlantic 1 data center and establishing operations at its Midwest-1 facility. Corvex expects the Mid-Atlantic 1 expansion and Midwest-1 to be ready for service in the fourth quarter of 2026. Corvex also holds a right of first refusal on an additional 12.5 MW at Midwest-1, which could bring Corvex’s total capacity above 20 MW.

 

●In early September 2026, Corvex closed a private placement of approximately 4.26 million shares of common stock at $7.75 per share, for gross proceeds of approximately $33 million.

 

●In August 2026, a Corvex subsidiary entered into a secured equipment financing facility with USD.AI and borrowed $7.5 million to fund GPU servers for the Blackwell expansion at Mid-Atlantic 1. The loan is secured by the subsidiary’s assets, including the related customer contract, is supported by a limited guaranty from Corvex’s operating subsidiary, and is non-recourse to Corvex, Inc.

 

●In August 2026, Corvex appointed Garrett Brams, formerly a managing director at Cushman & Wakefield, to lead data center sourcing and development for its AI Factory platform. Corvex also appointed Michael Craig as Vice President of Architecture and Site Operations in July. Craig, a former Crusoe leader, brings more than 25 years of experience across HPC architecture, GPU infrastructure and operations.

 

Capital Structure Update

 

As of November 10, 2026, the Company had approximately 39.1 million shares of Common Stock outstanding and approximately 21,800 shares of Series D Preferred Stock outstanding, convertible into approximately 21.8 million shares of Common Stock, or approximately 60.9 million common shares on an as-converted basis.

About Corvex

 

Corvex is an AI cloud computing company specializing in GPU-accelerated infrastructure for AI workloads. Corvex’s platform allows organizations to leverage the advantage of AI by providing secure, scalable, and cost-efficient computational resources. Corvex’s infrastructure leverages advanced GPU-accelerated compute clusters, high-throughput storage systems and layered architecture to provide enhanced security, consistent performance, and efficiency at scale. As previously announced on March 19, 2026, Corvex, Inc. (formerly known as Movano Inc.) acquired Corvex Legacy Holdings, Inc. (Corvex OpCo, formerly known as Corvex, Inc.) (such acquisition the “Merger”). Following the Merger, the Company was renamed Corvex, Inc., effective March 23, 2026.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

To supplement our consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”), we use adjusted EBITDA to help us evaluate our business. We use this non-GAAP financial measure to make strategic decisions, establish business plans and forecasts, identify trends affecting our business, and evaluate operating performance. We believe that this non-GAAP financial measure may be helpful to investors because it allows for greater transparency into what measures we use in operating our business and measuring our performance and enables comparison of financial trends and results between periods where items may vary independent of business performance. This non-GAAP financial measure is presented for supplemental informational purposes only, should not be considered a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with GAAP, and may be different from similarly titled non-GAAP measures used by other companies.

 

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net loss, excluding (i) depreciation and amortization, (ii) stock-based compensation, (iii) benefit from income taxes, (iv) transaction costs related to the Merger, (v) gain on disposal of assets and (vi) interest and other income, net. A reconciliation is provided below to reconcile adjusted EBITDA to net loss, the most directly comparable financial measure stated in accordance with GAAP. Corvex encourages investors to review the related GAAP financial measure and the reconciliation of the non-GAAP financial measure to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measure, and not to rely on any single financial measure to evaluate Corvex’s business.
