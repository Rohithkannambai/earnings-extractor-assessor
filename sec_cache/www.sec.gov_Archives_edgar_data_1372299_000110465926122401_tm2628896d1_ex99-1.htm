Ocugen Provides Business Update with Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Conference Call and Webcast Today at 8:30 a.m. ET

oExecuted agreement with local partner Global Alliance for Regenerative Medicine (GARM) to coordinate and manage patient care
oProgressing site readiness in preparation to accept patients
oAdvancing toward full approval and potential patient enrollment as early as year-end

 

·Executed an exclusive OCU400 license agreement for the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) with Roots Pharmaceutical

·Cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and short-term investments of $84.6 million as of September 30, 2026; with expected runway into 2028

 

MALVERN, Pa., November 4, 2026 -- Ocugen, Inc. (“Ocugen” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: OCGN), a pioneering biotechnology leader in gene therapies for blindness diseases, today reported third quarter 2026 financial results along with a general business update.

 

“With three Phase 3 modifier gene therapy programs underway, Ocugen is executing against a clear strategy of advancing our pipeline and leveraging strategic partnerships to expand global reach and potentially bring our therapies to patients worldwide,” said Dr. Shankar Musunuri, Chairman, CEO and Co-Founder of Ocugen. “As we demonstrated in the third quarter, we remain focused on executing key clinical and regulatory milestones, expanding our commercial opportunities, and maintaining a disciplined approach as we advance toward the anticipated OCU400 Phase 3 topline data early next year.”

 

Ocugen continues to advance its three late-stage modifier gene therapy programs toward key clinical and regulatory milestones, with OCU400 expected to be the first Phase 3 study to read out in the first quarter of 2027. The Company remains focused on execution across its pivotal programs while preparing for potential regulatory submissions and broader patient access opportunities.

 

Clinical Program Updates

 

OCU400 (RP)

·Granted Provisional Approval and Priority Designation from the Longevity and Regenerative Therapies (LARTA) Board of The Bahamas; agreement with local partner, GARM, to manage process; site readiness progressing; LARTA final approval and potential patient enrollment as early as year-end

 

·Completed an exclusive OCU400 license for MENA with Roots Pharmaceutical. Ocugen is eligible for up to $4 million in combined upfront license fee and potential initial development milestone payments, up to $255 million of potential sales milestones, and a 22% royalty on net sales, and will manufacture commercial supply. It is Ocugen’s second regional OCU400 license agreement, following Kwangdong Pharmaceutical in South Korea

 

·Top-line data from the 140-patient Phase 3 liMeliGhT trial are expected in the first quarter of 2027, followed by a planned Biologics License Application (BLA) submission; completed Process Performance Qualification (PPQ) batches to support the BLA and commercial launch supply

 

 

 

 

 

OCU410ST (Stargardt disease)

 

·Topline data from the Phase 2/3 pivotal confirmatory trial (N=63 subjects) are anticipated in the second quarter of 2027, with a planned BLA submission to follow

 

OCU410 (GA)

 

·Announced the first patient dosed in ArMaDa3, a 240-subject global Phase 3 trial randomizing patients 2:1 to a single subretinal injection of OCU410 or untreated control across the U.S., Canada, and Europe; the primary endpoint is GA lesion growth over 12 months

 

·Presented 12-month Phase 2 ArMaDa2 results at three scientific conferences, including two podium presentations at AAO 2026

 

Corporate Updates

 

•Named Jolanda Crombach General Manager of its newly established European affiliate in Amsterdam to support the OCU410 Phase 3 development program, upcoming marketing authorization submissions, and potential commercialization in Europe. Ms. Crombach most recently served as Managing Director and Board Member of Moderna Netherlands and previously held leadership roles at MSD (Merck) and Pfizer

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

·The Company’s cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and short-term investments totaled $84.6 million as of September 30, 2026, which is expected to extend the runway into 2028

 

·The Company had 339.3 million shares of common stock outstanding as of September 30, 2026

 

·Total operating expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $19.7 million and included research and development expenses of $12.5 million and general and administrative expenses of $7.2 million, compared to total operating expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2025, of $19.4 million that included research and development expenses of $11.1 million and general and administrative expenses of $8.2 million

 

·Ocugen reported a $(0.01) net loss per common share for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to a $(0.07) net loss per common share for the three months ended September 30, 2025

About Ocugen, Inc.

 

Ocugen, Inc. is a pioneering biotechnology company developing gene therapies for blindness diseases. The Company’s breakthrough modifier gene therapy platform has the potential to address significant unmet medical needs across large patient populations through a gene-agnostic approach. Unlike traditional gene therapies and gene-editing technologies that target a single gene mutation, Ocugen’s modifier gene therapies are designed to address the underlying disease biology by restoring balance across multiple gene networks. The Company is currently advancing programs for inherited retinal diseases and other causes of blindness that affect millions worldwide, including retinitis pigmentosa, Stargardt disease, and geographic atrophy, an advanced form of dry age-related macular degeneration. Discover more at www.ocugen.com and follow the Company on LinkedIn and X.
