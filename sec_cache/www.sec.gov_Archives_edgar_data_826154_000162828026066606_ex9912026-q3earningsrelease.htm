Orrstown Financial Services, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

•Net income of $22.8 million, or $1.17 per diluted share, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to net income of $21.2 million, or $1.09 per diluted share, for the three months ended June 30, 2026;
•Net interest margin, on a tax equivalent basis, increased by 25 basis points to 4.12% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 3.87% in the second quarter of 2026; excluding the amortization of the fair value mark on the redeemed subordinated notes, net interest margin was 4.00% (1) in the second quarter of 2026;
•Return on average assets was 1.60% and return on average equity was 14.62% for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to 1.53% and 13.96%, respectively, for the three months ended June 30, 2026;
•Total loans increased by $95.3 million, or 9% annualized, from June 30, 2026 to September 30, 2026; classified loans decreased by 8% during the third quarter of 2026; nonaccrual loans to total loans decreased to 0.54% at September 30, 2026 from 0.58% at June 30, 2026;
•Total deposits decreased by $38.1 million, or 3% annualized, from June 30, 2026 to September 30, 2026;
•Efficiency ratio was 56.0% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to 60.1% for the three months ended June 30, 2026;
•Federal income tax credits purchased resulted in a $0.4 million income tax benefit for the third quarter of 2026 compared to an income tax benefit of $1.6 million from tax credit purchases during the second quarter of 2026;
•The Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.30 per common share, payable November 10, 2026, to shareholders of record as of November 3, 2026.

HARRISBURG, PA (October 20, 2026) -- Orrstown Financial Services, Inc. (the "Company") (NASDAQ: ORRF), the parent company of Orrstown Bank (the “Bank”), announced earnings for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. Net income totaled $22.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to net income of $21.2 million and $21.9 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, respectively. Diluted earnings per share was $1.17 for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.09 and $1.13 for the three months ended June 30, 2026 and September 30, 2025, respectively.
Adam L. Metz, President and Chief Executive Officer, said, “Orrstown delivered strong third quarter results, with diluted earnings per common share of $1.17 and a return on average assets of 1.60%. The quarter demonstrated solid execution across the franchise, including a 25 basis point increase in net interest margin to 4.12%, a 56.0% efficiency ratio and 9% annualized loan growth. We also continued to see favorable trends in credit quality, with both classified loans and nonaccrual loans declining during the quarter. Our results reflect the progress we are making in growing core net interest income while actively managing funding costs and the declining benefit of purchase accounting accretion from our previous merger. Although the economic, interest rate and competitive environments remain dynamic, we enter the remainder of the year from a position of strength and are confident in our ability to execute across a range of economic conditions.”

DISCUSSION OF RESULTS
Balance Sheet
Loans
Loans held for investment increased by $95.3 million, or approximately 9% annualized, from $4.1 billion at June 30, 2026 to $4.2 billion at September 30, 2026. Commercial loans increased by $45.4 million, or approximately 6% annualized, from June 30, 2026 to September 30, 2026, with particularly strong growth in the commercial and industrial segment. Home equity lines of credit increased by $31.6 million, or approximately 36% annualized, which included purchases with a principal balance totaling $15.5 million. The retail teams have been successful in recent efforts over the past several quarters to drive growth in this category. First lien residential mortgages increased by $17.0 million, or approximately 13% annualized.
Investment Securities
Investment securities, all of which are classified as available-for-sale, decreased by $29.9 million to $919.4 million at September 30, 2026 from $949.3 million at June 30, 2026. During the three months ended September 30, 2026, net unrealized losses on investment securities increased by $41.6 million as a result of widening spreads and a rise in interest rates. The Bank purchased $34.9 million of investment securities, consisting of $24.9 million of agency mortgage backed securities and collateralized mortgage obligations and $10.0 million of non-agency collateralized mortgage obligations during the third quarter of 2026. The remaining change in investment securities during the quarter was due primarily to paydowns of $24.2 million, partially offset by net accretion of $1.4 million. The overall duration of the Company's investment securities portfolio was 4.2 years at September 30, 2026 compared to 4.5 years at June 30, 2026. See Appendix B for a summary of the Bank's investment securities at September 30, 2026, highlighting their concentrations and credit ratings.
Deposits
During the third quarter of 2026, deposits decreased by $38.1 million, or 3% annualized, and totaled $4.6 billion at both September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026. Time deposits, interest-bearing demand deposits, non-interest bearing deposits and savings deposits decreased by $28.1 million, $25.1 million, $24.8 million and $8.7 million, respectively. The decreases were primarily due to normal seasonal activity as well as time deposit maturities. These decreases were partially offset by a $48.6 million increase in money market deposits resulting from both promotional activities and the purchase of $25.1 million of brokered deposits. The Bank's loan-to-deposit ratio was 92% at September 30, 2026 compared to 89% at June 30, 2026.
Borrowings
The Company actively manages its liquidity position through its various sources of funding to meet the needs of its clients. FHLB advances and other borrowings were $417.3 million at September 30, 2026 compared to $274.8 million at June 30, 2026. The higher utilization of borrowings during the third quarter of 2026 was primarily to fund loan growth. The Bank seeks to maintain sufficient liquidity to ensure that client needs can be addressed in a timely basis. The Bank had available alternative funding sources, such as FHLB advances and other wholesale options, of $1.7 billion at September 30, 2026.

Income Statement
Net Interest Income and Margin
Net interest income increased by $4.5 million to $53.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $48.8 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The net interest margin, on a tax equivalent basis, increased by 25 basis points to 4.12% in the third quarter of 2026 from 3.87% in the second quarter of 2026. The yield on interest-earning assets increased by eight basis points and the cost of deposits decreased by five basis points during the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026. In addition, the net interest margin in the second quarter of 2026 includes the $1.6 million accelerated amortization expense of the subordinated notes' fair value mark. Excluding the amortization of the fair value mark on the redeemed subordinated notes, net interest margin was 4.00%(1) in the second quarter of 2026.
Interest income on loans, on a tax equivalent basis, increased by $3.2 million to $66.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $63.5 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. Average loans increased by $92.6 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026. Interest income on loans includes accretion on purchase accounting marks of $3.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $4.3 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. Prepayment fee income on loans increased by $335 thousand during the third quarter of 2026 compared to the second quarter of 2026.
Interest expense, on a tax equivalent basis, decreased by $1.5 million to $24.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $25.9 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. Borrowing costs decreased by $1.1 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026 primarily due to the accelerated amortization of the remaining subordinated debt fair value mark of $1.6 million during the second quarter of 2026.
The cost of deposits decreased by five basis points during the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026. Average interest-bearing deposits decreased by $27.9 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended June 30, 2026. Average FHLB advances and other borrowings increased by $130.6 million from the three months ended June 30, 2026 to the three months ended September 30, 2026 driven by loan growth in addition to maintaining sufficient liquidity due to seasonal deposit activity during the third quarter of 2026.
Provision for Credit Losses on Loans
The allowance for credit losses ("ACL") on loans increased to $46.8 million at September 30, 2026 from $46.6 million at June 30, 2026. The ACL to total loans was 1.11% at September 30, 2026 compared to 1.13% at June 30, 2026. The Company recorded provision expense on loans of $1.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $0.4 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The increase in provision expense resulted from strong loan growth, net charge-offs and updates to our qualitative factors, partially offset by a favorable GDP forecast and macroeconomic conditions. Net charge-offs were $1.0 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $1.2 million during the three months ended June 30, 2026.
Classified loans decreased by $4.0 million to $45.2 million at September 30, 2026 from $49.2 million at June 30, 2026 due to repayments of $5.3 million and charge-offs of $1.0 million, partially offset by net downgrades of $2.3 million. Nonaccrual loans totaled $22.9 million at September 30, 2026 compared to $23.8 million at June 30, 2026. The decrease of $0.9 million in nonaccrual loans was due to repayments of $1.5 million and net charge offs totaling $1.0 million, partially offset by loans placed on nonaccrual status of $1.6 million. Nonaccrual loans to total loans decreased to 0.54% at September 30, 2026 from 0.58% at June 30, 2026. Management believes the ACL to be adequate based on current asset quality metrics and economic forecasts.

Noninterest Income
Noninterest income decreased by $0.4 million to $13.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $13.8 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026.
Swap fee income decreased by $0.3 million to $0.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $0.7 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. Swap fee income will fluctuate based on market conditions and client demand.
Mortgage banking income decreased by $0.2 million to $0.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $0.4 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The decrease was due to a reduction in loan sale activity and a decrease in the fair value adjustment of loans held-for-sale and in the pipeline due to rising market interest rates.
Wealth management income was $6.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $5.9 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026.
Noninterest Expenses
Noninterest expenses decreased by $0.4 million to $37.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $37.7 million in the three months ended June 30, 2026.
Professional services expense decreased by $0.2 million to $1.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $1.2 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The Company continues to focus on reducing third-party utilization.
Other operating expenses decreased by $0.4 million to $3.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $4.3 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. This was due primarily to an increase of $0.5 million in mark-to-market gains on non-hedging derivatives from the second quarter of 2026 to the third quarter of 2026.
Salaries and benefits expense increased by $0.2 million to $22.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $22.2 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The third quarter of 2026 reflects a full quarter impact from the increase in merit-based salaries that went into effect in May 2026 and the impact of one extra day compared to the prior quarter.
Advertising and bank promotions expense decreased by $0.7 million to $0.4 million in the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $1.1 million in the three months ended June 30, 2026 due to $0.7 million in contributions to tax credit programs during the second quarter of 2026. Taxes other than income increased by $0.6 million to $1.0 million in the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $0.4 million in the three months ended June 30, 2026. This increase reflects the tax credits recognized in the second quarter of 2026 as result of the charitable contributions.
Income Taxes
The Company's effective tax rate was 19.4% for the third quarter of 2026 compared to 14.2% for the second quarter of 2026. The Company's effective tax rate for the three months ended September 30, 2026 is less than the 21% federal statutory rate primarily due to the purchase of federal income tax credits, which reduced income tax expense by $0.4 million. During the three months ended June 30, 2026, the Company purchased federal income tax credits, which reduced income tax expense by $1.6 million. In addition, the effective tax rate for both periods was impacted by tax-exempt income, including interest earned on tax-exempt loans and securities and non-taxable income from life insurance policies and tax credits partially offset by the disallowed portion of interest expense against earnings in association with the Bank's tax-exempt investments under the Tax Equity and Fiscal Responsibility Act of 1982. The Company regularly analyzes its projected taxable income and makes adjustments to the provision for income taxes accordingly.
Capital
Shareholders’ equity totaled $605.2 million at September 30, 2026 compared to $621.7 million at June 30, 2026. The decrease of $16.5 million is primarily due to other comprehensive losses of $34.5 million and dividends of $5.9 million, partially offset by net income of $22.8 million and share-based compensation activity of $1.1 million. The change in other comprehensive losses is primarily due to the increase in net unrealized losses on investment securities caused by the widening of spreads and a rise in interest rates.
Tangible book value per common share(1) was $25.96 at September 30, 2026 compared to $26.71 at June 30, 2026. The Company's tangible common equity ratio was 9.1% of total tangible assets at September 30, 2026 compared to 9.5% at June 30, 2026. Return on average tangible common equity per common share(1) was 17.52% for the three months ended

September 30, 2026 compared to 16.86% for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The decreases in tangible book value per common share and the tangible common equity ratio were primarily due to the decrease in shareholders' equity caused by the increase in net unrealized losses on investment securities.
The Company's tier 1 common equity, tier 1 capital and total risk-based capital ratios were 12.1%, 12.3% and 13.3%, respectively, at September 30, 2026 compared to 12.0%, 12.2% and 13.2%, respectively, at June 30, 2026. The Company's Tier 1 leverage ratio increased to 10.2% at September 30, 2026 compared to 10.1% at June 30, 2026.
At September 30, 2026, all four capital ratios applicable to the Company were above regulatory minimum levels to be deemed “well capitalized” under current bank regulatory guidelines. The Company continues to believe that capital is adequate to support the risks inherent in the balance sheet, as well as growth requirements.

About the Company
With $5.7 billion in assets, Orrstown Financial Services, Inc. and its wholly-owned subsidiary, Orrstown Bank, provide a wide range of consumer and business financial services in Berks, Cumberland, Dauphin, Franklin, Lancaster, Perry and York Counties, Pennsylvania and Anne Arundel, Baltimore, Harford, Howard, and Washington Counties, Maryland, as well as Baltimore City, Maryland. The Company’s lending area also includes counties in Pennsylvania, Maryland, Delaware, Virginia and West Virginia within a 75-mile radius of the Company's executive and administrative offices as well as the District of Columbia. Orrstown Bank is an Equal Housing Lender and its deposits are insured up to the legal maximum by the FDIC. Orrstown Financial Services, Inc.’s common stock is traded on Nasdaq (ORRF). For more information about Orrstown Financial Services, Inc. and Orrstown Bank, visit www.orrstown.com.
