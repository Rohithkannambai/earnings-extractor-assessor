Leatt Corporation Announces Results for

the Third Quarter 2026

 

Global revenues increase 43% to $20.46 million, highest quarterly revenues in four years; Net income increases 309% to $2.20 million

 

CAPE TOWN, South Africa, (November 12, 2026) – Leatt Corporation (OTCQB: LEAT), a leading developer and marketer of head-to-toe protective equipment for MOTO, MTB, ADV and a wide range of extreme and high-velocity sports, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. All financial numbers are in U.S. dollars.

 

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights

 

  ●Q3 2026 revenues increased 43% to $20.46 million, compared to $14.34 million in Q3 2025, the Company’s highest quarterly revenues in four years.

 

●Sales to global distributors increased 52%, dealer direct revenues increased 24% and consumer direct sales increased 25%.

 

●Q3 2026 net income increased 309% to $2.20 million, or $0.35 per basic and $0.34 per diluted share, compared to $0.54 million, or $0.09 per basic and $0.08 per diluted share, in Q3 2025.

 

 ●For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, revenues increased 23% to $56.36 million, compared to $45.89 million for the same period in 2025.

 

  ●For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net income increased 74% to $4.88 million, or $0.78 per basic and $0.76 per diluted share, compared to $2.80 million, or $0.45 per basic and $0.43 per diluted share, for the same period in 2025.

 

  ●Cash flows provided by operating activities were $4.59 million for the first nine months of 2026, compared to $1.45 million for the first nine months of 2025.

 

  ●Launched GRiTT by LeattTM, the Company’s first consumable product line, with initial shipments exceeding expectations.

 

  ●Launched two MTB shoes with unique SuperTackTM technology designed to maximize grip.

 

Chief Executive Officer Sean Macdonald commented, “The third quarter of 2026 was a strong quarter by nearly every measure. Total global revenues of $20.46 million were our highest in four years, up 43% over the third quarter of 2025. Sales to global distributors increased by 52%, dealer direct revenues increased by 24% and consumer direct sales increased by 25%. U.S. revenues increased by 24% to $5.33 million. International revenues increased by 50% to $15.12 million.

 

 

“Net income for the third quarter increased by 309% to $2.20 million as compared to the third quarter of 2025. Gross profit increased by 48% to $9.32 million, in part due to the recoupment of global trade tariffs levied on the importation of goods into the U.S.

 

“Our growth was broad-based across product categories. Body armor sales increased by $2.93 million, helmet sales increased by $2.25 million, and other products, parts and accessories sales increased by $1.04 million as compared to the third quarter of 2025. Only sales of our flagship neck brace declined slightly, by $0.11 million, against a strong prior-year quarter in which neck brace volumes grew 39%.

 

“In terms of marketing and product launches, the third quarter of 2026 was one of our busiest ever. We launched global campaigns across all three of our riding categories—MOTO, MTB and ADV—designed to support the sell-through we are seeing at dealers and distributors. We also launched our first consumable product line, GRiTT by LeattTM, a range of premium bike care products. This momentum continues into the fourth quarter of 2026. This week we launched two new MTB flat-pedal riding shoes featuring our unique SuperTackTM technology, designed to maximize grip and enhance the riding experience.

 

“For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, revenues increased 23% to $56.36 million, and net income increased 74% to $4.88 million. Consumer direct sales continued to be a highlight, increasing by 48% to $5.24 million. Gross profit increased by 27% and gross profit as a percentage of sales improved to 45% from 43% as compared to the same period in 2025.”

 

Founder and Chairman Dr. Christopher Leatt remarked: “Our latest product releases are the result of years of work by our design and engineering team, and they show the depth of the pipeline that we have been building across riding categories. In MOTO, our model year 2027 5.5 Racewear range introduced innovations such as HeatGripTM, a durable inner-knee material that replaces leather. In MTB, our SuperTackTM technology, featured in two new flat-pedal shoes, was refined through seven rubber compounds and five outsole designs before testing by more than 100 riders. And with GRiTT by LeattTM, we have entered the bike care market with our first consumable product line. Innovation that improves the riding experience has been at the heart of Leatt since day one.”

 

Summary of Third Quarter 2026 Revenues

 

Revenues for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $20.46 million, a 43% increase, compared to $14.34 million for the third quarter ended September 30, 2025. This increase in worldwide revenues was attributable to a $2.93 million increase in body armor sales, a $2.25 million increase in helmet sales and a $1.04 million increase in other products, parts and accessories sales, partially offset by a $0.11 million decrease in neck brace sales.

 

Revenues generated from sales to our customers in the U.S. increased by 24%, from $4.29 million to $5.33 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Revenues associated with international customers were $15.12 million and $10.06 million, or 74% and 70% of revenues for the quarters ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively.

 

Balance Sheet and Cash

 

Cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash increased by $2.27 million since year-end to $15.50 million at September 30, 2026. Cash flows provided by operations were $4.59 million for the first nine months of 2026, more than three times the $1.45 million for the same period in 2025.

 

Despite 23% revenue growth, inventory decreased by $1.60 million since year-end, reflecting strong sell-through and disciplined working capital management. The current ratio at September 30, 2026 was 4.7:1. The Company continued to return capital to shareholders through its share repurchase program, repurchasing $181,555 of common stock during the first nine months of 2026.

Business Outlook

 

Macdonald added: “We are on track for a solid year in 2026. While there is still the potential for geopolitical headwinds, we are optimistic that the traction we have gained will continue. Participation remains strong, and demand for our expanding line of innovative products continues to grow, fueling robust international re-ordering patterns and momentum in domestic sales at the dealer and consumer levels.

 

“Our 2026 results clearly show the progress that we are making. In the third quarter, revenues grew 43% while operating costs grew only 15%, lifting our net margin to 10.8% from 3.8% a year ago. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, helmet sales are up 48% and footwear revenues are up 67%.

 

“We believe that this momentum will be sustained as we continue to invest in building Leatt as a global consumer brand that riders are compelled to engage with. We are actively building out our global team of sales, marketing and brand management professionals and continuing to build a pipeline of innovative products. 

 

“The Leatt team just returned from our global distributor conference in Barcelona energized and enthusiastic. Our distributors, customers and business partners joined us and are excited by the momentum we are building in expanding our product pipeline, amplifying our brand and reaching a much wider riding audience.”

About Leatt Corporation

 

Driven by the science of thrill, Leatt Corporation develops head-to-toe personal protective gear for extreme and action sports. This includes the award-winning Leatt-Brace®, a neck brace system considered the gold standard for neck protection when worn in conjunction with a helmet. Leatt products are designed for participants in extreme sports that use motorcycles, bicycles, mountain bikes, all-terrain vehicles, snowmobiles, and other open-air vehicles. For more information, visit www.leatt.com.
