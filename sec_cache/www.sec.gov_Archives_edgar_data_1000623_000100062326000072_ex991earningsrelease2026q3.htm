Mativ Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results


ALPHARETTA, GA, November 4, 2026 -– Mativ Holdings, Inc. ("Mativ" or the "Company") (NYSE: MATV) reported financial results for the three-months ended September 30, 2026.

Mativ Third Quarter 2026 Highlights1

•Sales of $499.5 million decreased 2.8% year over year, and 2.3% on an organic basis, primarily due to the impact of the previously disclosed tornado
•GAAP loss of $1.2 million versus GAAP loss of $3.2 million in prior year period; GAAP EPS was $(0.03)
•Adjusted income was $17.5 million; Adjusted EPS was $0.31
•Adjusted EBITDA was $62.3 million, down 7% versus prior year period
•Year-to-date profitability and cash flow performance pacing ahead of strong prior year
◦Gross profit was $295.9 million, up 7% versus prior year
◦Cash provided by operating activities was $117.4 million, up 3% versus prior year period
◦Free cash flow was $90.5 million, up 5% versus prior year period




Management Commentary

“Mativ’s third quarter performance demonstrated continued momentum across the business, highlighted by the FAM segment’s strongest sales growth ever, driven by robust demand in Aerospace & Defense and Transportation,” said Shruti Singhal, Mativ's President and CEO. “Our disciplined execution — which positioned Mativ to deliver year-over-year third-quarter Adjusted EBITDA growth prior to the operational disruption from the July tornado in Wisconsin — and our strong cash generation give us confidence in delivering full-year financial performance ahead of the prior year.

“Our response to the tornado is further proof of Mativ’s resilience and execution capabilities. Recovery remains on plan and we expect inventory losses to be fully recoverable. The team's efforts to quickly restore customer supply have earned strong praise from our customers. I want to thank our crisis management team and all employees for their exceptional customer commitment and dedication while navigating this challenge.

“With this solid operational foundation in place, we are pivoting to growth and executing on initiatives that will accelerate sustainable, profitable revenue growth, driving increased customer and shareholder value.”

GAAP operating expenses decreased $0.6 million year-over-year, and adjusted unallocated expenses (EBITDA) (see non-GAAP reconciliations) increased $0.5 million versus prior year.

Cash Flow & Debt

Year-to-date 2026 cash provided by operating activities was $117.4 million. Capital spending totaled $26.9 million. Working capital was a $11.9 million source of cash due to the impact of an increase in accounts payable and other current liabilities and accrued income taxes, partially offset by an increase in accounts receivable and inventories.

Total debt was $976.4 million as of September 30, 2026, an improvement of $52.5 million year-over-year. Cash and cash equivalents was $97.3 million. The resulting net debt was $879.1 million. Total liquidity was approximately $373.5 million, consisting of $97.3 million of Cash and cash equivalents and $276.2 million of revolver availability. The Company's debt is expected to mature on a staggered basis through 2033.

About Mativ

Mativ Holdings, Inc. is a global leader in specialty materials, solving our customers’ most complex challenges by engineering bold, innovative solutions that connect, protect and purify our world. Headquartered in Alpharetta, Georgia, we manufacture on three continents and generate sales in over 100 countries through our family of business-to-business and consumer product brands. The company’s two operating segments, Filtration & Advanced Materials and Sustainable & Adhesive Solutions, target premium applications across diversified and growing categories. Our broad portfolio of technologies combines polymers, fibers and resins to optimize the performance of our customers’ products across multiple stages of the value chain. Our leading positions are a testament to our best-in-class global manufacturing, supply chain and materials science capabilities. We drive innovation and enhance performance, finding potential in the impossible.
