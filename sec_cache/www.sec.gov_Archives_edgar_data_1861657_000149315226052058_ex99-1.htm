Canton Strategic Holdings, Inc. Releases Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operational Results

 

Total revenue of $3 million, an increase of 104% since Q2, in the Company’s first full quarter of Super Validator and locking-as-a-service operations

 

NEW YORK, NOVEMBER 16, 2026 – Canton Strategic Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: CNTN) (“Canton Strategic” or the “Company”), the first publicly traded company to leverage Canton Coin (“CC”) to support the Canton Network’s ability to digitize traditional financial markets, today released financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

During the quarter, the Company recorded total revenue of $3 million, compared with approximately $1.5 million in the second quarter of 2026, an increase of 104%. Revenue consisted of $1.5 million of network validation revenue from its Super Validator (“SV”) operations and $1.5 million of other revenue, primarily from its locking-as-a-service (“LaaS”) offering. Net loss was $(35,078,297), compared with $(19,254,826) in the second quarter of 2026. Adjusted EBITDA was $610,414, the Company’s first positive quarterly Adjusted EBITDA, compared with $(820,449) in the second quarter of 2026. Total operating expenses were $2,710,641, an increase of 0.5% from the second quarter, and total revenue was 112.6% of total operating expenses, compared with 55.4% in the prior quarter. Revenue growth of 104% outpaced the 3.1% increase in Adjusted Operating Expenses to $2.7 million and the Adjusted Operating Expenses Coverage Ratio rose to 112.6% from 56.9% in the prior quarter.

 

“We are proud to have achieved our first positive Adjusted EBITDA this quarter, a notable milestone as we mark the first year of operations since our launch in November 2025,” said Mark Wendland, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Canton Strategic Holdings. “With a full year under our belt as a publicly traded company operating our CC strategy, we remain focused on growth. Our Super Validator operations and locking-as-a-service offering allow us to support the Canton Network and its participants while also earning revenue that we can deploy for continued investment in the ecosystem and enhanced value for shareholders. With our operating cost base largely fixed and clear visibility to additional approved Super Validator weight, we believe the business is positioned for significant operating leverage and margin expansion.”

Business Update

 

Super Validator Operations

 

The Company operates a Super Validator on the Canton Network, earning CC based on its approved weight, which functions like a share count: a higher weight means a proportionally larger claim on the CC minted to Super Validators in each network round. Active minting began on May 9, 2026. On September 30, 2026, the Company’s active weight was 6.5, in line with the unlock schedule disclosed in its second quarter results. The remaining weight is expected to unlock through the first quarter of 2028, as shown below, subject to the Company meeting deliverables under CIP-0102 and continuing to satisfy the CC holdings requirement under CIP-0114, and Canton Network review and approval processes.

Locking-as-a-Service

 

Canton Network governance requires that Super Validators and Featured Applications lock a minimum amount of CC to maintain their status as network participants. Through the Company’s LaaS offering, launched on April 23, 2026, the Company is compensated for locking its own CC on a client’s behalf while retaining legal and beneficial ownership. Compensation takes the form of a daily interest rate on locked CC, a share of the customer’s network rewards, or an equity grant earned over the contract term. Only a wallet identifier, not custody or title, is shared with the Canton Foundation for compliance verification.

 

The third quarter was the first full quarter of LaaS operations. Other revenue, which consists primarily of LaaS, was $1,547,994, compared with $1,304,633 in the second quarter, an increase of 18.7%.

 

Canton Network Update

 

The third quarter saw significant momentum across the Canton Network, evidenced by the July completion of DTCC’s tokenization pilot and the approval of six new Super Validators representing a global array of institutional firms. Additionally, the International Securities Lending Association (ISLA) and International Swaps and Derivatives Association (ISDA) joined the Canton Foundation this quarter, gaining the ability to shape the governance of the network.

 

“As institutions prepared for the next phase of DTCC’s tokenization efforts, we saw an increased focus by many of the world’s largest financial institutions on building on the Canton Network, whether through governance activities as we saw through ISLA and ISDA joining the Foundation, or Super Validator operations as was the case with a16z crypto, LSEG, Shinhan Asset Management, among others,” continued Wendland. “As we look to the end of the calendar year, we remain focused on supporting these institutions and others as they capitalize on the increased collateral mobility and capital efficiencies gained through blockchain rails.”

 

Subsequent to quarter end, the DTCC launched its Tokenization Service on October 28, 2026, marking a turning point in the adoption of blockchain rails by financial institutions. Additionally, on October 23, 2026, CIP-0129, “Universal Burn and Synchronizer Pricing” was approved, which is expected to bring production traffic on dedicated synchronizers into the Canton Coin burn-and-reward economy, in place of the historical license-fee model, while leaving CC issuance and the reward allocation split unchanged. Volume pricing and collateral-backed commitment discounts are to be set by a subsequent CIP. Additional burn without a change to the issuance schedule could improve the burn-to-mint ratio; the effect will depend on actual traffic, discounts and implementation.

 

Financial statements

 

Non -GAAP Measures of Financial Performance

 

In addition to financial measures presented under generally accepted accounting principles in the United States of America (“GAAP”), the Company evaluates performance using non-GAAP financial measures including adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA”), Adjusted Operating Expenses and Adjusted Operating Expenses Coverage Ratio.

 

 
 

 

Adjusted EBITDA

 

The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss), excluding net income (loss) from discontinued operations, income tax provision (benefit), stock-based compensation expense and unrealized gains or losses on digital asset holdings. Management believes this financial measure provides a performance measurement that reflects our core business operations. Adjusted EBITDA is provided in addition to, and should not be considered a substitute for, GAAP financial measures.

 

Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as a financial measure, should be considered supplemental in nature, and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for net loss, net loss from continuing operations, cash flows from operating activities or other financial measures prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Adjusted EBITDA may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies.

 

Management considers Adjusted EBITDA together with, and not as a substitute for, the Company’s GAAP financial results and other measures of financial performance and liquidity. Investors should review the reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA to its most directly comparable GAAP financial measure presented below.

 

Adjusted Operating Expenses

 

Adjusted Operating Expenses is defined as GAAP total operating expenses minus stock-based compensation expense, which the Company believes is not indicative of its ongoing expenses. The amount and timing of the excluded items are unpredictable, are not driven by core results of operations, and render comparisons with prior periods less meaningful.

 

Adjusted Operating Expenses Coverage Ratio

 

Adjusted Operating Expenses Coverage Ratio is calculated as total revenue divided by Adjusted Operating Expenses. The Company also presents total revenue as a percentage of total operating expenses, which is calculated using GAAP total operating expenses. Management believes the Adjusted Operating Expenses Coverage Ratio is useful to investors because it indicates the extent to which revenue covers the Company’s operating expenses, excluding stock-based compensation.

About Canton Strategic Holdings, Inc.

 

Canton Strategic Holdings, Inc.
