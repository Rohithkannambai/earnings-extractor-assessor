Kodiak Gas Services Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results, Increases Full Year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA and Discretionary Cash Flow Guidance

THE WOODLANDS, Texas — November 9, 2026 — Kodiak Gas Services, Inc. (NYSE: KGS) (“Kodiak” or the “Company”) today reported financial and operating results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company also increased full year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA and discretionary cash flow guidance.
Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights
•Record Compression Infrastructure(1) segment revenues of $318.4 million
•Compression Infrastructure segment gross margin percentage of 47.5% and adjusted gross margin percentage(2) of 70.0%
•Reported Power Infrastructure(1) segment revenues of $42.0 million, a 27.6% increase compared to the second quarter of 2026
•Power Infrastructure segment gross margin percentage of 65.8% and adjusted gross margin percentage(2) of 75.2%
•Net income and adjusted net income(2) attributable to common shareholders of $71.5 million, or $0.70 per diluted share(2)
•Record adjusted EBITDA(2) of $224.5 million, a 28.5% increase compared to third quarter 2025
•Quarterly net cash provided by operating activities of $196.3 million and discretionary cash flow(2) of $158.5 million, a 35.9% increase compared to third quarter 2025
•Declared a quarterly dividend of $0.51 per share, a 4.1% increase over the second quarter of 2026
•Added 102 thousand horsepower of new, large compression units to revenue generating horsepower
•Achieved Compression Infrastructure fleet utilization of 98.3%, a 70 basis point increase compared to third quarter 2025, and Power Infrastructure fleet utilization of 92.5%
•Announced agreement to provide 76 megawatts of behind-the-meter, baseload power capacity, including balance of plant, to a West Texas data center
Revised 2026 Guidance
•Increased Adjusted EBITDA guidance to a range of $840 million to $860 million and discretionary cash flow guidance to a range of $580 million to $600 million
•Reduced Power Infrastructure segment growth capital expenditures to reflect updated expectations for the timing of new equipment deliveries, and slightly increased Compression Infrastructure segment growth capital expenditures to reflect incremental equipment deliveries


(1)The Company previously managed its business through two operating segments: Contract Services and Other Services. CEO Commentary
"Our third quarter results reflect the continued strength and profitability across our core business segments," said Mickey McKee, Kodiak's President and Chief Executive Officer. "Both our Compression Infrastructure and Power Infrastructure segments delivered exceptional financial results. In Compression Infrastructure, we delivered our third consecutive quarter of 70% adjusted gross margins, as increased operating efficiencies, driven by our focus on technology and cost discipline, offset inflationary pressures. In Power Infrastructure, our adjusted gross margin of 75.2% demonstrates the premium value customers place on reliable, scalable power solutions.
“The sizable sequential revenue increase in our Power Infrastructure segment is a direct reflection of the robust market demand for our assets — our gensets are realizing meaningful increases in pricing and, importantly, are staying on location longer, driving greater revenue visibility and returns. We recently achieved a significant milestone in our Power Infrastructure by commencing initial site work and beginning equipment setting at our data center project in West Texas, which will provide stable revenue in 2027. Given the high visible demand for large horsepower compression and the robust commercial pipeline of power projects, we remain highly confident in the long-term trajectory of both platforms and are focused on executing against our strategic priorities to drive sustainable value for our shareholders."
Segment Information
Compression Infrastructure segment revenue was $318.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, a 7.2% increase compared to $297.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. Compression Infrastructure segment gross margin was $151.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, a 10.8% increase compared to $136.4 million in the third quarter of 2025 and adjusted gross margin was $222.8 million in the third quarter of 2026, a 9.9% increase compared to $202.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Power Infrastructure segment revenue was $42.0 million in the third quarter of 2026. Gross margin and adjusted gross margin were $27.6 million and $31.6 million, respectively, in the third quarter of 2026.
Other Services segment revenue was $41.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, a 60.6% increase compared to $25.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. Other Services segment gross margin and adjusted gross margin were each $4.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, a 23.5% increase compared to $3.8 million for each measure in the third quarter of 2025.
Financial Results
Net income attributable to common shareholders was $71.5 million or $0.70 per share, in the third quarter of 2026.
Long-Term Debt and Liquidity
Total debt outstanding was $2.8 billion as of September 30, 2026. The Company had $1.6 billion available on its ABL Facility and $1.7 billion in total liquidity including cash on the balance sheet as of September 30, 2026. Kodiak’s credit agreement leverage ratio was 3.1x for the third quarter of 2026.
Share Repurchase Program Extension
On November 4, 2026, the Company's Board of Directors extended the expiration date of the Company's share repurchase program to December 31, 2027. The Company has approximately $31.7 million available for share repurchases under its share repurchase program.

About Kodiak
Kodiak is a leading contract compression, distributed power and energy infrastructure services provider in the United States. Headquartered in The Woodlands, Texas, Kodiak partners with customers across the energy and digital infrastructure sectors, including oil and gas producers, midstream operators and data center developers and operators, to enable the safe, reliable, and efficient production of energy that powers our critical energy future. Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Adjusted net income and adjusted earnings per share are considered non-GAAP measures. Adjusted net income is defined as net income adjusted to exclude certain items, as applicable, such as (i) impairment of long-lived assets; (ii) severance expenses; (iii) transaction expenses; (iv) sales tax reserve; (v) loss on disposal of business; (vi) loss (gain) on derivatives; (vii) loss on extinguishment of debt; and (viii) the tax effects of the adjustments. Adjusted earnings per share is calculated by dividing adjusted net income by the weighted average diluted shares outstanding.
Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA percentage are considered non-GAAP measures. Adjusted EBITDA is defined net income before interest expense; income tax expense; and depreciation and amortization; plus certain items, as applicable, such as (i) impairment of long-lived assets; (ii) loss (gain) on derivatives; (iii) equity compensation expense; (iv) severance expenses; (v) transaction expenses; (vi) sales tax reserve; (vii) loss (gain) on disposal of business; (viii) loss (gain) on sale of assets; and (ix) loss on extinguishment of debt. We define adjusted EBITDA percentage as adjusted EBITDA divided by total revenues.
Adjusted net income, adjusted diluted EPS, adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA percentage are used as supplemental financial measures by our management and external users of our financial statements, such as investors, commercial banks and other financial institutions, to assess: (i) the financial performance of our assets without regard to the impact of financing methods, capital structure or historical cost basis of our assets; (ii) the viability of capital expenditure projects and the overall rates of return on alternative investment opportunities; (iii) the ability of our assets to generate cash sufficient to make debt payments and pay dividends; and (iv) our operating performance as compared to those of other companies in our industry without regard to the impact of financing methods and capital structure. We believe adjusted net income, adjusted diluted EPS, adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA percentage provide useful information because, when viewed with our GAAP results and the accompanying reconciliation, they provide a more complete understanding of our performance than GAAP results alone. We also believe that external users of our financial statements benefit from having access to the same financial measures that management uses in evaluating the results of our business. Reconciliations of adjusted net income and adjusted EBITDA to net income and adjusted diluted EPS to GAAP diluted earnings per share, the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures are presented below.
Adjusted gross margin is defined as revenue less cost of operations, exclusive of depreciation and amortization expense. Adjusted gross margin percentage is defined as adjusted gross margin divided by total revenues. We believe adjusted gross margin and adjusted gross margin percentage are useful as supplemental measures to investors of our operating profitability. Discretionary cash flow is considered a non-GAAP measure. Discretionary cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less (i) maintenance capital expenditures; (ii) certain changes in operating assets and liabilities; and (iii) certain other expenses; plus certain items, as applicable, such as (w) severance expenses; (x) transaction expenses; and (y) sales tax reserve. We believe discretionary cash flow is a useful liquidity and performance measure and supplemental financial measure in assessing our ability to pay cash dividends to our stockholders, make growth capital expenditures and assess our operating performance. Free cash flow is considered a non-GAAP measure. Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less (i) maintenance capital expenditures; (ii) certain changes in operating assets and liabilities; (iii) certain other expenses; (iv) growth capital expenditures; and (v) other capital expenditures; plus certain items, as applicable, such as (w) severance expenses; (x) transaction expenses; (y) sales tax reserve; and (z) proceeds from sale of assets.
