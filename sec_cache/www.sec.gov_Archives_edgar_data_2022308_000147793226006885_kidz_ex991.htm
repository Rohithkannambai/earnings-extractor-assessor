EXHIBIT 99.1

 

KIDZ AI Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

•

Revenue Increased 40% Sequentially; Gross Margin Improved to 65.9%; Net Loss Narrowed 74% Sequentially

 

 

•

Net Cash Position of Approximately $14.9 Million, or Approximately $14.53 per Share – Approximately 12x Market Capitalization

 

 

•

Repurchased aggregate of 74,082 shares of Class B common stock at an average price of approximately $3.36 per share, for a total of approximately $249,194.

 

NEW YORK CITY, NY / ACCESS Newswire / November 12, 2026 / KIDZ AI Inc. (NASDAQ: KIDZ) (NASDAQ: KIDZW) (“KIDZ AI” or the “Company”), an education technology company advancing AI infrastructure and GPU compute initiatives alongside its core EdTech operations, today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

•

Revenue Growth and Strategic Transition: Third-quarter revenue increased 40% sequentially, marking the Company’s first quarter-over-quarter revenue increase in 2026. The Company continues to advance its GPU computing infrastructure strategy, with initial hardware procurement and deployment planning underway.

 

 

•

Net Loss: Net loss of $654,057 compared to net income of $2,520,989 in the prior year quarter.

 

 

•

Net Cash Position: Net cash position of approximately $14.9 million – comprised of cash and cash equivalents and USDC stablecoin holdings, less principal amount of notes payable or approximately $14.53 per share as of September 30, 2026. Based on the November 11, 2026 closing price of $1.17 per share, the Company’s net cash position represented approximately 12 times its market capitalization.

 

 

•

Share Repurchases: During the quarter, the Company repurchased an aggregate of 74,082 shares of its Class B common stock at an average purchase price of approximately $3.36 per share, for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $249,194.

 

 

•

Improved Profitability: Gross margin improved to 65.9% from 43.9% in the second quarter of 2026.

 

 

•

Strengthened Balance Sheet: Stockholders’ equity increased approximately 349% to $16.9 million, while total liabilities decreased approximately 54% to $5.4 million compared with December 31, 2025. Notes payable decreased approximately 69% to $2.6 million. The total liabilities-to-equity ratio improved from 3.12x to approximately 0.32x.

Management Commentary

 

“2026 marks an important transition for KIDZ AI as we expand beyond our traditional education technology business into AI computing infrastructure and Physical AI,” said Stephanie Luo, Chief Executive Officer of KIDZ AI Inc. “We are focused on building a foundation across these areas through infrastructure investments, technology development, and strategic industry relationships, while continuing to advance our core education business.”

 

“We are pursuing this transformation while strengthening our financial position. Our improved balance sheet, increased stockholders’ equity, and reduced liabilities provide a stronger financial foundation as we evaluate and pursue new business opportunities. Our focus remains on responsible capital allocation and building sustainable long-term value for shareholders.”

Use of Non-GAAP Information

 

“Net cash position” is not a financial measure calculated in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). The Company defines “net cash position” as cash plus USDC stablecoin holdings, less the outstanding principal amount of notes payable. Management believes this measure provides useful supplemental information regarding the Company’s liquidity, but it should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the most directly comparable GAAP measures.
