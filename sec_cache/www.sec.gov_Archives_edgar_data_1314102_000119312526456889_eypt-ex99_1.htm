EyePoint Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Recent Corporate Developments

– Organizational focus on DURAVYUTM Phase 3 DME program; trials fully enrolled with topline data expected in early Q4 2027 –

– DURAVYU DME program supported by the positive Phase 2 VERONA trial and multi-MOA activity blocking both VEGF and inflammatory IL-6 pathways involved in disease pathogenesis –

– Strategic prioritization expected to generate $30 million in annualized savings –

– $118 million in cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments, as of September 30, 2026 –

WATERTOWN, Mass., November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – EyePoint, Inc. (Nasdaq: EYPT), a company committed to developing and commercializing innovative therapeutics to improve the lives of patients with serious retinal diseases, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and highlighted recent corporate developments.

“The recent Phase 3 wet AMD data demonstrated DURAVYU’s ability to control fluid and maintain retinal anatomy through Week 56, along with a favorable safety profile that included every six-month redosing. This important data supports our strategic decision to prioritize our resources on DME, where we believe there is strong scientific and clinical rationale for DURAVYU as a durable treatment option for this vision threatening exudative disease.” said Jay S. Duker, M.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of EyePoint. “Our conviction in the DME program is further grounded in the positive Phase 2 VERONA trial results, the multi-MOA activity for DURAVYU addressing the distinct biology of DME driven by both inflammatory pathways as well as vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF), the substantial clinical experience established with DURAVYU, and the opportunity to evaluate the program in two fully enrolled, well-controlled pivotal trials within a year.”

In the Phase 2 VERONA clinical trial in DME, DURAVYU 2.7 mg met the primary endpoint and demonstrated an early and sustained improvement in vision and anatomical control, including a +7.1-letter improvement in best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) and a 76-micron reduction in central subfield thickness (CST) reduction at Week 24. Seventy-three percent of DURAVYU 2.7 mg patients remained supplement-free through Week 24 versus 50% of aflibercept-treated patients in the control arm. The Phase 3 COMO and CAPRI clinical trials are fully enrolled with over 550 patients and topline data expected early in the fourth quarter of 2027.

R&D Updates:

DURAVYU (vorolanib intravitreal insert)

Diabetic Macular Edema (DME)

•
On July 30, 2026, we announced the pivotal Phase 3 COMO and CAPRI clinical trials completed enrollment, with a total of over 550 patients now enrolled in five months.
o
The identical non-inferiority trials versus an on-label aflibercept control include every six-month re-dosing and follow a clear and recognized regulatory approval pathway.
•
Topline data for both DME trials are anticipated early in the fourth quarter of 2027.

Wet Age-Related Macular Degeneration (Wet AMD)

•
On August 17, 2026, we announced the Phase 3 LUGANO trial did not achieve the primary endpoint of statistical non-inferiority in mean change in BCVA versus on-label 2 mg aflibercept control in the full analysis population, despite demonstrating visual improvement.
o
DURAVYU achieved key secondary endpoints including a 42% reduction in treatment burden achieving superiority versus on-label aflibercept (nominal p-value of <0.0001), with 54% of eyes supplement-free and 79% receiving zero or one supplement up to Week 56, as well as strong anatomic control.
o
DURAVYU maintained a favorable safety profile with repeat dosing in patients. There were no observed occurrences of insert migration, anterior chamber opacities, free-floating drug particles, or retinal vasculitis.
•
On October 19, 2026, we announced the Phase 3 LUCIA trial did not achieve the primary endpoint of statistical non-inferiority in mean change in BCVA versus on-label 2mg aflibercept control in the full analysis population, despite demonstrating visual improvement.
o
DURAVYU achieved key secondary endpoints including a superior 38% reduction in treatment burden (nominal p-value <0.0001), with 52% of eyes supplement-free and 71% receiving zero or one supplement up to Week 56, as well as strong anatomic control, consistent with the first Phase 3 LUGANO clinical trial.
o
DURAVYU maintained a favorable safety profile with repeat dosing in patients. There were no observed occurrences of insert migration, anterior chamber opacities, free-floating drug particles, or retinal vasculitis.
•
In a pooled analysis of the greater than 50% of patients in both trials who were supplement-free up to Week 56, DURAVYU was observed to be statistically non-inferior in mean change in BCVA versus on-label 2 mg aflibercept control.
•
We will conclude the wet AMD program before completion of the full second year of the Phase 3 studies for both LUGANO and LUCIA, while also allowing additional patients to complete year two to support the necessary safety database for a potential new drug application for DME.
•
We will complete additional analyses of both the LUGANO and LUCIA data along with the pooled Phase 3 datasets to further assess next steps for DURAVYU as a potential treatment for wet AMD.

Recent Corporate Updates

·
Following the LUCIA results, our Board of Directors approved a strategic prioritization designed to extend our financial resources and focus on the late-stage DME program. The resulting approximately 53% workforce reduction, as well as associated operational adjustments, are expected to generate an estimated annualized savings of approximately $30 million.

 

“Capital discipline is now one of our highest priorities,” said George O. Elston, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer. “Our objective is to preserve the resources required to obtain the pivotal DME data, while delivering COMO and CAPRI to the utmost standard. We have multiple levers available to us to further extend our financial runway and we intend to actively evaluate and pursue those opportunities to preserve strategic flexibility and maximize long-term shareholder value.”

Review of Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, total net revenue was $0.8 million compared to $1.0 million for the corresponding period in 2025. Operating expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, totaled $97.6 million versus $63.0 million for the corresponding period in 2025. This increase was primarily attributable to ongoing DURAVYU Phase 3 clinical trials for wet AMD and DME.

Net non-operating income totaled $1.3 million and net loss was $95.6 million, or ($1.07) per share, compared to a net loss of $59.7 million, or ($0.85) per share, for the corresponding period in 2025.

Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026, totaled $118 million compared to $306 million as of December 31, 2025.

Financial Outlook

We expect the cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026, together with the additional $10 million of capital raised subsequent to quarter-end pursuant to our “at-the-market” equity facility and with anticipated operational adjustments, will enable us to fund operations into late third quarter of 2027. We continue to pursue business development, financing and other cost reduction opportunities that could further extend this cash runway.

About EyePoint

EyePoint, Inc. (Nasdaq: EYPT) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company committed to developing and commercializing innovative therapeutics to improve the lives of patients with serious retinal diseases. The Company’s lead product candidate, DURAVYU™, is an innovative investigational sustained delivery treatment for serious retinal diseases combining vorolanib, a selective and patent-protected tyrosine kinase inhibitor, with EyePoint’s proprietary bioerodible Durasert E™ sustained-delivery technology. DURAVYU is currently being evaluated in Phase 3 pivotal trials for diabetic macular edema (DME).

The Company is committed to partnering with the retinal community to improve patient lives while creating long-term value, with four approved drugs over three decades and tens of thousands of eyes treated with EyePoint innovation.

EyePoint is headquartered in Watertown, Massachusetts, with a commercial manufacturing facility in Northbridge, Massachusetts.

Vorolanib is licensed to EyePoint exclusively by Equinox Sciences, a Betta Pharmaceuticals affiliate, for the localized treatment of all ophthalmic diseases outside of China, Macao, Hong Kong and Taiwan.

DURAVYU™ has been conditionally accepted by the FDA as the proprietary name for EYP-1901. DURAVYU is an investigational product; it has not been approved by the FDA. FDA approval and the timeline for potential approval is uncertain.
