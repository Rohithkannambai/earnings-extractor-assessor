CVR Energy Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

  •  

Separately announced today a definitive agreement to acquire Marathon Petroleum Corporation’s St. Paul Park and Mandan refineries and related marketing business

 

  •  

Third quarter 2026 net income attributable to CVR Energy stockholders of $570 million; Adjusted EBITDA of $582 million

 

  •  

$253 million liability removed from balance sheet due to the EPA’s August 2026 decision granting a full waiver for Wynnewood Refining Company, LLC (“WRC”) for the 2025 compliance period

 

  •  

Declared cash dividend of 10 cents for the third quarter 2026

 

  •  

CVR Partners, LP (“CVR Partners”) announced a third quarter 2026 cash distribution of $1.79 per common unit

SUGAR LAND, Texas (November 3, 2026) – CVR Energy, Inc. (“CVR Energy” or the “Company”) (NYSE: CVI) today announced its third quarter 2026 results including a net income attributable to CVR Energy stockholders of $570 million, or $5.67 per diluted share, and an adjusted earnings per diluted share of $4.00, compared to net income attributable to CVR Energy stockholders of $374 million, or $3.72 per diluted share, and an adjusted earnings per diluted share of 40 cents for the third quarter of 2025. Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $563 million compared to net income of $401 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was $582 million compared to $180 million for the third quarter of 2025.

“We are incredibly excited about our announcements this morning of a definitive agreement to acquire two high quality refineries from Marathon Petroleum, along with the associated marketing and logistics assets,” said Dane Neumann, CVR Energy’s President and Chief Executive Officer. “We also announced the best quarterly results in the Company’s history on both a consolidated basis and in the Petroleum segment, driven by strong operations across our system and record crack spreads. We are also pleased to announce a quarterly dividend of 10 cents per share. Looking ahead, we remain optimistic about the near-term outlook while we seek to close on our transformative transaction with Marathon Petroleum by the end of the year.”

“CVR Partners’ results for the third quarter were impacted by the planned turnaround at its East Dubuque facility, which was completed in mid-October as scheduled,” Neumann continued. “Despite the downtime associated with the turnaround, CVR Partners had another quarter of solid results and declared a cash distribution of $1.79 per common unit for the third quarter.”

Non-GAAP Measures

Our management uses certain non-GAAP measures, and reconciliations to those measures, to evaluate current and past performance and prospects for the future to supplement our financial information presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). These non-GAAP measures are important factors in assessing our operating results and profitability and include the measures defined below.

The following are non-GAAP measures we present for the periods ended September 30, 2026 and 2025:

EBITDA - Consolidated net income (loss) before (i) interest expense, net, (ii) income tax expense (benefit) and (iii) depreciation and amortization expense.

Petroleum EBITDA and Nitrogen Fertilizer EBITDA - Segment net income (loss) before segment (i) interest expense, net, (ii) income tax expense (benefit), and (iii) depreciation and amortization.

Refining Margin - The difference between our Petroleum Segment net sales and cost of materials and other.

Adjusted Refining Margin - Refining Margin adjusted for certain significant noncash items and items that management believes are not attributable to or indicative of our underlying operational results of the period or that may obscure results and trends we deem useful.

Refining Margin and Adjusted Refining Margin, per Throughput Barrel - Refining Margin and Adjusted Refining Margin divided by the total throughput barrels during the period, which is calculated as total throughput barrels per day times the number of days in the period.

Direct Operating Expenses per Throughput Barrel - Direct operating expenses for our Petroleum Segment divided by total throughput barrels for the period, which is calculated as total throughput barrels per day times the number of days in the period.

Adjusted EBITDA, Petroleum Adjusted EBITDA, and Nitrogen Fertilizer Adjusted EBITDA - EBITDA, Petroleum EBITDA, and Nitrogen Fertilizer EBITDA adjusted for certain significant non-cash items and items that management believes are not attributable to or indicative of our underlying operational results of the period or that may obscure results and trends we deem useful.

Adjusted Earnings (Loss) per Share - Earnings (loss) per share adjusted for certain significant non-cash items and items that management believes are not attributable to or indicative of our on-going operations or that may obscure our underlying results and trends.

Free Cash Flow - Net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less capital expenditures and capitalized turnaround expenditures.

We present these measures because we believe they may help investors, analysts, lenders and ratings agencies analyze our results of operations and liquidity in conjunction with our U.S. GAAP results, including but not limited to our operating performance as compared to other publicly traded companies in the refining and fertilizer industries, without regard to historical cost basis or financing methods and our ability to incur and service debt and fund capital and turnaround expenditures. Non-GAAP measures have important limitations as analytical tools, because they exclude some, but not all, items that affect net earnings and operating income. These measures should not be considered substitutes for their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures. See “Non-GAAP Reconciliations” included herein for reconciliation of these amounts. Due to rounding, numbers presented within this section may not add or equal to numbers or totals presented elsewhere within this document.

Factors Affecting Comparability of Our Financial Results

Our results of operations for the periods presented may not be comparable with prior periods or to our results of operations in the future for the reasons discussed below.

Petroleum Segment

Major Scheduled Turnaround Activities - Total capitalized turnaround expenditures as part of planned turnarounds were $4 million and $165 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively, and $5 million and $189 million during the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively.

Renewable Fuel Standard - Based on the August 31, 2026 EPA decision document issued to the Company’s subsidiary, WRC, granting a 100 percent waiver from its RFS obligation for the 2025 compliance period (the “August 2026 SRE Decision”), WRC’s obligation for the 2025 compliance period was reduced by more than 118 million RINs, representing approximately $253 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. The August 2026 SRE Decision follows the EPA’s August 22, 2025 decision document issued to WRC, which granted full and partial waivers for WRC’s RFS obligation for the 2019 through 2024 compliance periods (the “August 2025 SRE Decision”) and reduced WRC’s RFS obligations by more than 424 million RINs, representing approximately $488 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Nitrogen Fertilizer Segment

Major Scheduled Turnaround Activities - We incurred turnaround expenses of $27 million and $1 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively, and $29 million and $2 million during the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively.
