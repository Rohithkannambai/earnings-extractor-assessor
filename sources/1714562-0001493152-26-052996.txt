GameSquare Holdings Reports 2026 Third Quarter Results
with Revenue up 77% YoY and Net Income Reaches $10.1 Million
Third quarter net income of $10.1 million, or $0.79 per diluted share driven by gains on digital assets and improvements in operating profitability
Second consecutive quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA drives year-to-date adjusted EBITDA of $0.4 million, an improvement of nearly $6.8 million year-over-year
Company enters seasonally strong fourth quarter with continued commercial momentum, expanding growth opportunities, and expected contributions from FanEngine acquisition and broader IP strategy
November 16, 2026, FRISCO, TX – GameSquare Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: GAME), (“GameSquare”, or the “Company”), today announced financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.
Justin Kenna, CEO of GameSquare stated, “Momentum continues to build across GameSquare as the improvements we have made to our operating model translate into stronger underlying profitability. We believe we have turned an important corner in 2026, and we are well positioned to deliver sustained operating profitability as we further scale our platform, expand our capabilities and benefit from recent strategic investments.”
Kenna continued, “Our commercial progress throughout the third quarter and into the fourth quarter reflects expanding brand relationships, new strategic partnerships, and growing opportunities across our creator, technology and media businesses. Highlights include our partnership with Azuki, the expansion of Hungryboy Hot Sauce into Walmart’s online marketplace, and our strategic partnership with Chartis to enhance gaming and creator campaign measurement. We also introduced Hatchet’s AI-powered creator intelligence platform, while FaZe Esports captured another major international championship. These developments demonstrate the breadth of our platform and our ability to create new opportunities across brands, creators and premium intellectual property.”
“As we enter our seasonally strong fourth quarter, we are encouraged by several growth catalysts, including the recent closing and integration of the FanEngine acquisition and the rollout of our expanded IP strategy. While the timing of certain customer engagements and disruptions related to the conflict in the Middle East impacted third-quarter revenue, we believe the expected fourth-quarter contribution from FanEngine, together with continued momentum across our core businesses, positions us well to achieve our previously announced pro forma guidance. We continue to opportunistically monetize our ETH holdings, prioritizing cash and stablecoins to reduce volatility and enhance financial flexibility. With a stronger operating foundation and expanding growth opportunities, we are confident in our ability to build on this momentum and create long-term shareholder value,” concluded Kenna.

Reverse Stock Split
On August 24, 2026, GameSquare completed a 1-for-8 reverse stock split of its common stock. All share and per-share amounts presented in this release have been retroactively adjusted to reflect the reverse stock split, unless otherwise noted.
Stock Repurchases
For the first nine months of 2026, GameSquare repurchased 1,389,339 shares of its common stock for $5.0 million, representing an average price of approximately $3.60 per share. To date in the fourth quarter of 2026, GameSquare repurchased an additional 214,026 shares of its common stock for $0.6 million, representing an average price of approximately $2.78 per share.
As of November 13, 2026, GameSquare has repurchased approximately 1,603,365 shares of its common stock for nearly $5.6 million, representing an average price of approximately $3.49 per share since the Company’s repurchase program started in October 2025. The Company has approximately $9.4 million remaining under its current authorization.

About GameSquare Holdings, Inc.
GameSquare (NASDAQ:GAME) is a cutting-edge media, entertainment, and technology company transforming how brands and publishers connect with Gen Z, Gen Alpha, and Millennial audiences. With a platform that spans award-winning creative services, advanced analytics, and FaZe Esports, one of the most iconic gaming organizations, we operate one of the largest gaming media networks in North America. As a digital-native business, GameSquare provides brands with unparalleled access to world-class creators and talent, delivering authentic connections across gaming, esports, and youth culture. Complementing our operating strategy, GameSquare has developed an innovative treasury management program designed to generate yield and enhance capital efficiency, reinforcing our commitment to building a dynamic, high-performing media company at the intersection of culture, technology, and next-generation financial innovation.

Management’s use of Non-GAAP Measures
This release contains certain financial performance measures, including “EBITDA” and “Adjusted EBITDA,” that are not recognized under accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“GAAP”) and do not have a standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP. As a result, these measures may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies. For a reconciliation of these measures to the most directly comparable financial measures calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP, see the section entitled “Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Measures” below.
We believe EBITDA is a useful measure to assess the performance of the Company as it provides more meaningful operating results by excluding the effects of expenses that are not reflective of our underlying business performance and other one-time or non-recurring expenses. We define “EBITDA” as net income (loss) before (i) depreciation and amortization; (ii) income taxes; and (iii) interest expense.
Adjusted EBITDA
We believe Adjusted EBITDA is a useful measure to assess the performance of the Company as it provides more meaningful operating results by excluding the effects of expenses that are not reflective of our underlying business performance and other one-time or non-recurring expenses. We define “Adjusted EBITDA” as EBITDA adjusted to exclude extraordinary items, non-recurring items and other non-cash items, including, but not limited to (i) share based compensation expense, (ii) transaction costs related to merger and acquisition activities, (iii) arbitration settlement reserves and other non-recurring legal settlement expenses, (iv) contract exit costs, primarily comprised of employee severance resulting from integration of acquired businesses, (v) impairment of goodwill and intangible assets, (vi) gains and losses on extinguishment of debt, (vii) change in fair value of assets and liabilities adjusted to fair value on a quarterly basis, (viii) gains and losses from discontinued operations, and (ix) net income (loss) attributable to non-controlling interest.
